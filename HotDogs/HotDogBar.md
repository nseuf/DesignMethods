### Nic Specialty Hotdog - Millionaire's Dog
#### Ingredients
Bun
Hot Dog
Truffle Oil
Gold Leaf
Garlic
Smoked Gouda
#### Assembly
Take Bun and inlay the smoked gouda as a base
Put hotdog in the bun
Sprinkle garlic and truffe oil on top
Finish with a bit of gold leaf
