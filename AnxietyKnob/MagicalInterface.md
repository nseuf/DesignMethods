Throughout the interview process many things stood out to me in my partner's daily life. He pointed out that he didn't like traveling back and forth from his house to campus multiple times per day, the fact that he leaves his dog home alone, or even his anxiety and the constant worry in his mind. The point I decided to make a magical interface around was his Anxiety, or how he described 
"My brain just runs on and on, sometimes I just need it to be quiet ... But I don't want it quiet forever, I like ideating and my ADHD is helpful for that"
So I focused my interface on that, making a portable knob that allows the user to have control over ones inner monologue. 

![drawing](AnxietyKnob/Images/IMG_20260901_204043.png)

The quick sketch just allowed me to get a few ideas down, what I landed on was not just a knob but something like a timer. The user can turn the knob all the way to the max, quieting the mind, but like an egg timer the knob will always go back to the default state. This introduces some balance to the design, allowing for a gradual reintroduction of the thoughts. For the display of the interface I had taken inspiration from some analog kitchen timers that display the time remaining with a red circle
